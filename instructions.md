# Instructions

## What this is

Public website for The Just Us League Limited, an Illawarra charity. One static page: `index.html`.

## Voice

Copy follows the Just Us League brand voice: neighbourly, checkable, hands-on, calm. Public name is Just Us League. First mention of the legal name is The Just Us League Limited. Do not use JUL in public sentences.

Say Illawarra and Wollongong for positioning. Do not advertise monthly meets. That program is cancelled.

The Australian AI Society is a program of the League, not a partner. Partners are organisations with their own governance.

Family kits go home. The classroom kit stays a charity asset. Say which.

On the language-model work: name whose knowledge it is and who gave permission when that is settled. Until then, keep the quieter, permission-first wording already on the page. Do not use preserve, revitalise, or ancient.

Public contact is service@jul.org.au. ABN in public copy is 50 603 437 467.
